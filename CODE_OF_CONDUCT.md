## Our standard

Participants in this repository are expected to communicate respectfully,
constructively, and in good faith. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal
attacks, harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and deliberate disruption
are not.

## Scope

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and
other project spaces used for contribution or support.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to `support@maatify.dev` with the relevant
context. Do not include credentials or unrelated sensitive information. Reports
will be reviewed and handled according to the project's applicable governance,
authorization, and license arrangements.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may clarify, moderate, or restrict participation when
necessary to protect a respectful and productive project space.
