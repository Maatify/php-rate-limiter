### Identifier note: `DEC-023`

The pinned standards material uses the label `DEC-023` for a **decision of the central standards repository**,
`MANDATORY_PRE_RELEASE_STRICT_LEAD_ACCEPTANCE_DECISION.md`, not for this repository's
[DEC-023](DEC-023_HOST_MODE_CONTRACT_AND_MODE_ELIGIBILITY.md) (Host mode contract). This applies to
`.agents/skills/maatify-strict-release-acceptance/SKILL.md` lines 15 and 39, and to
`docs/php-engineering-standards/standards/ai/AI_COLLABORATION_WORKFLOW_AR.md` line 1787. Decision preflight (Decision
Governance §7) keeps the two apart: a `DEC-023` cited by those files is the standards-repository decision, and a `DEC-023`
in this index is the local record. Those files are pinned and are not edited here. This note is removed once the reference
is qualified upstream and adopted through a pinned upgrade.
